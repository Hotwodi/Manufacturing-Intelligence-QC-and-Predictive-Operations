Manufacturing Intelligence, QC & Predictive Operations

Odoo 18 AI-Powered

A comprehensive AI-powered manufacturing intelligence suite for Odoo 18 that unifies production line monitoring, quality control, predictive maintenance, KPI dashboards and AI-driven defect analysis into a single application.

Key Features

  • Production Line Monitoring — Track capacity, throughput, OEE targets and AI-generated efficiency scores across every line.
  • Quality Inspections — Record incoming, in-process and final inspections with AI defect prediction and pass/fail/rework workflows.
  • Predictive Maintenance — Receive AI-driven failure predictions with risk levels, confidence scores and preventive/corrective/predictive scheduling.
  • KPI Dashboard — Monitor OEE, availability, performance, quality rate, scrap rate and on-time delivery with AI trend predictions.
  • Defect Analysis — Identify root causes with AI confidence scoring, track occurrence counts and manage corrective actions through to resolution.

Why Choose This Module?

Manufacturing Intelligence, QC & Predictive Operations brings AI-driven decision support to the factory floor. By consolidating production monitoring, quality control and maintenance into one integrated Odoo application, teams reduce downtime, improve quality and move from reactive to predictive operations.
